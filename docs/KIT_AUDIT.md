# Kit audit

Session S01 · 18 November 2026 · Review of the launch packet and development kit delivered 17 November 2026.

This audit records what was checked, what agrees, and what needs a decision before or during the foundation build. It changes none of the kit's specifications. Findings that need a choice are carried into the [foundation planning brief](planning/FOUNDATION_PLANNING_BRIEF.md).

Both CSV files use RFC 4180 CRLF line endings. `.gitattributes` stores them byte-for-byte (`*.csv -text`).

## Workbook

The workbook was generated by `planning/build_workbook.mjs`, which depends on OpenAI's proprietary `@oai/artifact-tool` runtime. It cannot be regenerated with this repository's toolchain. [WORKBOOK.md](../planning/WORKBOOK.md) asks the team to "choose one working record during execution". Recommendation: the repository JSON is the working record for engineering; the workbook remains a founder snapshot until a replacement generator is chosen (planning brief, Q12).

## What was not checked

Domain availability, trademarks, company registries and social handles (the packet also leaves these unverified); real Ghost(Pro), Buffer, Supabase or Vercel accounts (none exist); native Excel execution of the workbook. No runtime behavior of the planned application exists to test.
