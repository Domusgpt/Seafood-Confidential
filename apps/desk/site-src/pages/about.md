Seafood is a working world: boats and farms, buying desks and cold stores, kitchens and coastal communities. Its choices reach far beyond the fish counter.

Seafood Confidential was founded by Paul Phillips and a fellow seafood industry veteran to make that world easier to understand. We bring decades of experience to the conversation and invite people across fisheries, aquaculture, trade, science, policy, and food to add their own knowledge.

We publish reported features, practical explainers, first-person accounts, and interviews. A writer's perspective should be clear, commercial relationships should be disclosed, and readers should be able to follow the evidence behind a claim. When we make a material mistake, we correct the record.

## What we promise

Help readers understand a real seafood decision, the people affected, and the evidence behind it.

The heart of it is working knowledge made public: how a buyer judges a shipment, how a farmer makes a production decision, how a rule changes a working day, how a market develops, and how those choices reach the plate. Personal access starts the story; reporting and clear attribution make it trustworthy.

## Where to find things

Stories appear in six sections: On the Water, At the Farm, The Trade, Rules and Reality, At the Table, and Voices. Each story is labelled with its format, and opinion and sponsored work are always marked. [How we work](/how-we-work/) explains our standards, including how we label stories written with AI agents.
