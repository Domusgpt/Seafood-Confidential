Workbook

The workbook was generated by planning/build_workbook.mjs, which depends on OpenAI's proprietary @oai/artifact-tool runtime. It cannot be regenerated with this repository's toolchain. WORKBOOK.md asks the team to "choose one working record during execution". Recommendation: the repository JSON is the working record for engineering; the workbook remains a founder snapshot until a replacement generator is chosen (planning brief, Q12).
