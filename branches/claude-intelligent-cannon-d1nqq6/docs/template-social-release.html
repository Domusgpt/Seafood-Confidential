Social release checklist

Seafood Confidential · One record per post or clearly identified batch

Use this after the article's central claims are stable. The MVP allows agents to draft copy and propose assets. A human approves and schedules or publishes in Buffer. Preparing this record does not authorize an external send.

Release record

Field Required entry
Content ID Parent article or episode
Social item ID Stable ID, such as SC-2026-001-LI-01
Parent version Approved article version or published update date
Channel and account Exact destination; distinguish company account and personal account
Purpose Introduce a useful insight, explain evidence, or invite a relevant conversation
Canonical URL Verified destination article or episode
Tracking Agreed campaign parameters, if used; no private information in URLs
Copy version Exact text being approved
Asset version Exact image, video, audio, or carousel file
Proposed time Date, time, named timezone and UTC instant if scheduling
Reviewer Named human
Release owner Human who will use Buffer

Three repeatable formats

Insight and link

Lead with a concrete question or useful finding. Explain why it matters to a named seafood role. Link to the fuller piece. Avoid a claim stronger than the article supports.

Draft pattern: “What sits between a landed price and a delivered price? Our new feature follows the costs and decisions a buyer needs to understand. [One checked insight from the finished piece.] Read the full explanation: [link].”

This is a copy pattern, not a claim that the example article has been reported or published.

Evidence or excerpt

Choose one checked quotation, a clearly attributed finding, or a simple visual based on verified data. Retain the context needed to understand it. Record the source or timestamp. Do not turn a nuanced answer into a categorical claim to fit a card.

Practitioner question

Ask a question someone with relevant experience can answer. Invite specific examples without requesting private commercial information or personal data.

Example: “Which question do you wish more buyers asked before comparing seafood prices?” A question can stand alone or link to the feature; it does not need a provocative assertion.

Review before scheduling

  • The text agrees with the approved article or episode version.
  • Names, roles, species, geography, dates, units, and numbers are correct.
  • Any quote matches the recording or original text and preserves the relevant context.
  • Opinions, illustrative examples, sponsorship, and generated imagery are identified where relevant.
  • Media rights cover this channel and use; credits and guest permissions are recorded.
  • The image or video is legible on a phone; captions and alternative text are supplied where supported.
  • The URL resolves to the intended destination; the preview title and image are accurate.
  • The destination account and scheduled time are correct.
  • The item is not already scheduled or published; the stable social item ID has been checked.
  • The parent article has no unresolved correction affecting this copy.

Approval and release

Record approved exact version; reviewer; approval time; destination; release owner. Any material change to the wording, asset, destination, or timing returns to the human reviewer. Use native Buffer approval features when the selected plan supports them; otherwise the named reviewer records approval here and the human release owner schedules directly.

Never treat “draft created” as “approved” or “approved” as “published.” Record the scheduled platform item ID after scheduling and the public URL after successful publication. If delivery fails or the result is uncertain, inspect the existing item before retrying so a manual retry does not create duplicate posts.

After publication

Check the live text, image, link, and account. Log the public URL and any change. A human replies to reader comments and direct messages in the MVP; agents may propose responses for review.

If the source article is corrected, check all related queued and published social items. Amend, withdraw, or follow up as appropriate, recording the reason and actual action. Keep an accurate history of the correction rather than silently marking the task complete.

Capacity rule

Use up to three posts per selected channel per week. Start with LinkedIn. Adding a channel requires a named owner and realistic time allowance. A second channel receives adapted copy and media suited to its audience, not an automatic promise that every draft can be published everywhere.
