# Guest contributor onboarding

Seafood Confidential · Reusable intake and commissioning record

Use this for a guest essay, field dispatch, interview, or media contribution. It is an operational record. It does not replace the contributor's explicitly accepted publication and rights terms.

## Invitation copy

Subject: An invitation to contribute to Seafood Confidential

Hello [name],

We are building Seafood Confidential to bring more working knowledge into the seafood conversation. Paul Phillips and his cofounder are inviting people across fisheries, aquaculture, trade, science, policy, and food to explain the decisions and experiences they know well.

We would like to explore a contribution about [specific subject or reader question]. This could be a short article, a field dispatch, or a recorded conversation that we adapt into a feature. We can help shape the piece and prepare it for readers who know the industry as well as those who are learning it.

Before you begin, we will agree the scope, deadline, byline, any payment, and how the material may be used. Please tell us about relevant business relationships and any material you do not own or cannot authorize us to publish.

Would [specific angle] be a useful place to start?

[Named editor]

This is draft correspondence for a human to personalize and send. No invitation is sent by completing the template.

## Intake record

| Field | What to capture |
| --- | --- |
| Contributor identity | Full name, preferred byline, pronunciation and relevant credentials |
| Contact | Working contact stored in the restricted editorial record |
| Affiliation | Employer, organization, independent role, or other accurate description |
| Biography | A short public biography; confirm its wording with the contributor |
| Proposed subject | One question or experience the contribution will explain |
| Firsthand basis | Why the contributor is positioned to discuss this subject |
| Format | Article, dispatch, interview, audio, photographs, or other agreed format |
| Interests | Relevant employment, commercial relationships, investments, sponsorship, advocacy or funding |
| Prior publication | Where this material has already appeared and any restrictions |
| Accessibility needs | Recording, communication or document requirements the editor should accommodate |
| Assigned editor | Named human responsible for follow-through |

## Agree the commission

Create an article brief and assign a content ID. Confirm the deadline and timezone, approximate length or recording duration, revision process, compensation and expenses, and whether the contribution may also be used in the newsletter, podcast, social posts, or promotional excerpts.

Record the **actual accepted terms**, including permitted formats and territories, exclusivity if any, attribution, editing rights, and restrictions. A reasonable starting proposal is a clearly described nonexclusive publication arrangement with retained author ownership; the final terms require explicit agreement. Do not infer permission for audio, video, translations, clips, or advertising from permission for a written article.

Store the acceptance record and any release with the editorial file. If a guest supplies company photos, confirm who can authorize their use. A publicly visible image is not automatically available for republication.

## Access to the publication

A human editor invites a writer using Ghost's native **Contributor** role. This lets the writer create and edit their own draft posts without permission to publish or edit other writers' drafts. Confirm the assigned role before sending the invitation. Give the writer only the access needed for the commission.

Provide the agreed brief, this contributor guide, brand voice guidance, and the deadline. The contributor sends the editor the draft link when ready for review; the team should not assume an unconfigured review notification exists. The managing editor handles publication.

An interview guest does not need a Ghost account merely to take part. Collect guest details and recording permissions through the agreed editorial process.

## What to submit

- The draft or recording materials, with clear byline and affiliation.
- Source links and supporting records for factual claims.
- Exact quotations with speaker attribution and timestamps where relevant.
- Original media and its permission, credit, caption, and alternative text.
- Relevant interests and proposed disclosure.
- A short list of any unresolved facts, restrictions, or concerns.

Label firsthand recollection as firsthand recollection. If a case is fictional, illustrative, or a composite, say so. Disclose material AI assistance and identify passages or translations needing careful human verification. Do not upload confidential third-party material to an agent without the editor's explicit authorization and an appropriate configured workflow.

## Review and publication

The editor works with the contributor on clarity, accuracy, and scope. A request to check a fact or quotation does not create an automatic right to approve every editorial choice. Agree any special review conditions in advance.

Record the contributor's factual corrections, the editor's final approval, the publication date, and the published URL. Share the result with the contributor through a human-sent message. Any subsequent correction enters the same corrections process as founder-written work.

## Completion record

Record **commission accepted; rights accepted; access issued; draft received; review completed; publication approved; URL delivered; compensation completed if applicable; access reviewed**. Review or remove unused access according to the team's account policy.
